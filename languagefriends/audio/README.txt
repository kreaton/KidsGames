Language Friends - spoken letters, numbers and words

These recordings are synthetic speech, made with Microsoft text-to-speech (edge-tts)
in the Audio project (WordAudioGenerator). The voices are not human.

They are part of the Language Friends game and may only be used in it. Do not publish,
share or collect them as an audio dataset, and do not use them to train, fine-tune
or improve any text-to-speech or voice model.

<language>/alphabet/A01.mp3 ...   each letter of alphabets.csv, in order
<language>/numbers/N01.mp3 ...    the numbers 1-10
<language>/dictionary/D001.mp3 .. the picture words of dictionary.csv
